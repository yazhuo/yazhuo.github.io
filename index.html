About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Systems Group at ETH Zurich, where I am fortunate to work with Dr. Ana Klimović. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Emory University, advised by Dr. Ymir Vigfusson.

My research aims to make distributed systems more efficient and reliable, with a focus on caching, performance diagnosis, and cloud infrastructure. I investigate how these systems respond to changing workloads and failures, from controlling retries in microservices to diagnosing LLM serving failures and adapting web caches to AI-generated traffic.

I am looking for research and engineering opportunities in industry, particularly in distributed systems and AI infrastructure.

Research Focus

  • AI Serving & Model Deployment (ongoing)
    • Model onboarding, serving correctness, and hardware/runtime compatibility
    • Agent-assisted diagnosis, repair, and optimization of LLM deployments
  • Reliable Distributed Systems & Performance Diagnosis
    • Retry admission and control in service meshes; retry storms and metastable failures [NSDI’27]
    • Distributed tracing and causal modeling of end-to-end latency [SoCC’23]
    • Elastic cloud infrastructure, scheduling, and sandboxed execution [SOSP’25]
  • Caching & Data Infrastructure
    • Simple, efficient cache eviction [NSDI’24 🏆] [SOSP’23] [HotOS’23]
    • Workload characterization and web cache design for AI-generated traffic [SoCC’25]
